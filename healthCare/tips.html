Everyday health tips, in plain language.

No fads, no jargon — just small, repeatable habits our own clinicians recommend most often. Sorted by the part of your routine they touch.

Nutrition

Fill half your plate with vegetables before adding anything else.

Drink a glass of water before reaching for a snack — thirst often mimics hunger.

Keep fruit visible on the counter; you tend to eat what you see first.

Swap one soda a day for sparkling water with citrus.

Movement

Take a 10-minute walk after meals to help regulate blood sugar.

Stand up and stretch every hour if you sit for work.

Two short walks beat one long one you keep postponing.

Split heavy grocery loads into two trips instead of one — your back will thank you.

Sleep

Keep a consistent wake time, even on weekends.

Dim screens an hour before bed — blue light delays melatonin release.

A cool, dark room (around 65°F / 18°C) supports deeper sleep.

Caffeine after 2pm can still affect how easily you fall asleep at night.

Mental Wellness

Name the feeling before reacting to it — it often shortens its grip.

Ten minutes outside can reset a stressful afternoon.

Write down three things that went fine today, not just what didn't.

Asking for help early is easier than waiting until it becomes a crisis.
