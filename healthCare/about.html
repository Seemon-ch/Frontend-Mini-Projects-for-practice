Care built around people, not paperwork.

Willow Health started as a single room and a waiting list. Eighteen years later, we're still built around the same idea: good care starts with actually knowing the person in front of you.

’07

Founded by Dr. Ines Calloway in a converted storefront, with one exam room and a secondhand desk.

What began as a family practice grew because patients kept asking for the same things: someone who remembered their last visit, appointments that didn't take three weeks to book, and honest answers instead of a rushed handoff.

Today Willow Health is a full practice — physicians, a nutrition lead, and a mental health counselor working from the same patient notes, so nothing gets lost between visits or specialties.

We're still in the same neighborhood. We've just added more rooms.

What guides how we work

Four commitments that shape every appointment, not just the mission statement.

Whole-person care

A sore knee and a stressful month are rarely unrelated — we treat them that way.

Community first

Most of our patients live within walking distance. We plan around that, not around it being convenient for us.

Honest guidance

If a treatment isn't worth the cost or time, we'll say so — even if it's not what you came in expecting.

Preventive by default

We'd rather catch something early on a routine visit than treat it later as an emergency.
